<p>Hayek Wallet is an open-source, self-custodial Bitcoin wallet. A new wallet is created only from your own dice rolls – you supply the randomness yourself and can verify the process independently.</p>
<p><b>⚠ Experimental software. Use it with small amounts only and always write your recovery words on paper.</b></p>
<p><b>KEYS AND BACKUP</b></p>
<ul>
<li>24 words (BIP39) from 99 or more rolls of a six-sided die – the same method as Coldcard and SeedSigner</li>
<li>Optional passphrase with a clear explanation</li>
<li>Backup check: all 24 words, in random order, with suggestions</li>
<li>Words encrypted in Android Keystore (StrongBox where the phone has it)</li>
<li>Standard BIP84 addresses – restore in any common wallet</li>
</ul>
<p><b>PRIVACY</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Connect to your own node (Electrum server – Fulcrum, ElectRS), or deliberately choose a public server</li>
<li>The app never connects anywhere on its own</li>
<li>Your node's certificate is pinned</li>
<li>No accounts, analytics, ads or telemetry</li>
<li>KYC / no-KYC coin tags so coins don't get mixed by accident</li>
<li>Dust attack protection</li>
</ul>
<p><b>PAYMENTS</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Coin control and coin freezing</li>
<li>Fee bumping (RBF), payment requests with an amount, address book</li>
<li>Lightning through your own LND node</li>
<li>Incoming payment notifications</li>
</ul>
<p><b>MULTIPLE WALLETS AND COLDCARD</b></p>
<ul>
<li>A pocket wallet for everyday spending and a watch-only wallet for savings</li>
<li>Sign payments with a Coldcard hardware wallet over NFC – the keys never leave it</li>
<li>Watch-only mode (zpub / ypub / xpub or descriptor)</li>
</ul>
<p><b>SECURITY</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Unlock and confirm payments with fingerprint or PIN</li>
<li>Auto-lock, screenshots blocked</li>
<li>Backup of labels (BIP-329) and Lightning channels</li>
</ul>
<p>The app is free and available in English and Czech. Source code: https://github.com/milahajek/Hayek.wallet</p>
<p>Requires Android 11 or later and a screen lock. Lightning requires your own LND node.</p>
